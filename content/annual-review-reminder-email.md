# Annual review reminder — email copy

Working copy for the MailerLite automation behind the `annual-review` list
(the capture in the `#minimum` section of `index.html`). Not a web page — this file
is here so the wording lives in the repo next to the sheet it points at.

- **List / segment:** `annual-review`
- **Trigger:** subscriber joins the list → confirmation sends immediately; the
  reminder then sends every 12 months on the anniversary of joining.
- **Attachment / link:** `review-sheet.html` (or the PDF exported from it).
- **From:** the company owner, personally — not "the Venue-Ready team".
- **Rules:** no countdown, no fear, no upsell in the yearly email. It is free, it stays
  free, and it goes to people who have never bought anything. Keep it that way.

---

## Email 1 — sent immediately on signup

**Subject:** Your annual review reminder is set

**Preview text:** One email a year, and the walk-through sheet to go with it.

Hello,

That's your annual review reminder set. Twelve months from today we'll send you one
email with a walk-through sheet for reviewing your Martyn's Law procedures — and
that is the only thing this list is for.

If you'd like the sheet now, it's here: [the 20-minute annual review sheet](https://venue-ready.co.uk/review-sheet.html)

Reviewing your procedures is requirement 6 of the seven that standard-tier venues
have. It doesn't mean submitting anything to anyone. It means walking round once a
year, checking the plan still fits the building, and writing down what you found.
Twenty minutes, and often the honest answer is "nothing has changed".

You don't need to have bought anything from us to stay on this list, and you never will.

## Email 2 — the yearly reminder (sends every 12 months)

**Subject:** Time for your 20-minute review

**Preview text:** Walk round, tick seven things, file it. Often the answer is "no changes".

Hello,

A year ago you asked us to remind you when your Martyn's Law procedures were due a
review. This is that email.

Here's the sheet: [the 20-minute annual review sheet](https://venue-ready.co.uk/review-sheet.html)

Print it, walk round the building with it, and tick as you go:

- Has anything changed about how the building is used?
- Is the named responsible person still the right person?
- Are your notification details still accurate?
- Do the four procedures still fit the actual building — exits, invacuation room,
  locks, who calls 999?
- Does everyone on duty still know what to do, including people who started this year?
- Are your hirers and anyone you share the building with still in the loop?
- Is it filed, and is the next date in the diary?

Then sign the bottom and put it with your fire log. If nothing has changed, write
"no changes" and sign it — that is a complete and perfectly good review.

Do it sooner than a year if something material changes: a building alteration, a new
event much bigger than anything before, a change of responsible person, or anything
that made you think "our plan wouldn't have worked there".

That's everything. See you in another twelve months.

[Owner name]
Venue-Ready
venue-ready.co.uk
