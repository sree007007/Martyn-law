The Mis-selling Checklist

Martyn's Law has attracted sharks. Before you buy from anyone — including us — here are five claims that should make you walk away from a provider. Tick them off before you spend a penny.

1

"Compliance certificate"

No certificate exists in law. Nobody can certify you compliant — not us, not anyone. Training records? Useful. Certificates of compliance? Fiction. Walk away.

2

"Official" or "Government-approved"

The Home Office and the SIA endorse no product or provider. Any provider implying otherwise is misleading you. Walk away.

3

"You need CCTV, barriers or guards"

Not at standard tier. The Government's own MythBuster says procedures, not equipment. Anyone selling you hardware to "comply" is selling you something you don't need. Walk away.

4

Wrong numbers

"Applies from 100 capacity", "Tier 1" — the real thresholds are 200 (standard) and 800 (enhanced). Sellers who get the basics wrong shouldn't be writing your procedures. Walk away.

5

Countdown-timer panic

Spring 2027 is real; the panic isn't. The SIA has said it will be supportive and proportionate. Anyone selling fear is selling for themselves. Walk away.
